About this model and its limitations

This six-class model was evaluated on a controlled FS-Jump3D held-out split: 65.97% video-level accuracy and 0.652 macro F1. It expects one already-trimmed jump. It does not locate jumps in full programs, classify combinations, count rotations, or score GOE or jump quality. Performance on broadcast, internet, or phone video is not established.
